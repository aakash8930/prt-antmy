The Anatomy
of a Descent

One vessel. Six thousand metres. A world beyond daylight.

01 · Before the water

Built around
what must hold.

The first component is not the hull. It is the promise at its centre: a pressure chamber engineered to keep one atmosphere intact while the ocean applies six hundred.

Structure
Titanium rib frame
Design load
60 MPa

02 · An instrument, assembled

Every part has
one reason to exist.

Vision, ballast, propulsion, sampling and signal gather around the chamber. Nothing is ornamental. Every gram must earn the energy required to carry it down—and bring it back.

Independent systems
18
Dry mass

03 · The final seal

The surface closes
behind us.

The last hatch is not simply shut. It is measured, cleaned, seated and witnessed. Beyond this point, the vessel becomes a world with its own air, heat and time.

There is no small decision once the water is overhead.

04 · Leaving daylight

Down is the only
direction left.

Blue becomes violet. Violet becomes black. The vessel falls at twenty-five metres a minute while the familiar world withdraws above it.

Sunlight
0.01%
Temperature

05 · Four thousand metres

The ocean tests
every seam.

Metal contracts. Glass carries the weight of a city block. The smallest sound travels through the frame and becomes impossible to ignore.

The capsule does not resist the deep. It learns to carry it.

06 · Life below light

Darkness was never
empty.

Without the sun, life writes its own signals. Cold blue pulses travel through the water—warnings, invitations and names we have not learned to read.

07 · The seabed

At the limit,
something answers.

A mineral chimney rises where the maps predicted an empty plain. It is younger than the ocean, taller than the vessel and alive with heat from beneath the crust.

to look closer.

Vessel specification

Four systems.
One atmosphere.

01Pressure chamber+

A ribbed titanium frame distributes external pressure around a compact spherical chamber. The circular viewport uses a tapered acrylic block that seats more firmly as pressure rises.

  • Grade 5 titanium
  • Three-person environment
  • Acoustic stress monitoring
02Observation array+

Low-light optical sensors work beside the main viewport, recording colour and movement without flooding the environment with artificial light.

  • 4K low-light optics
  • Macro sample camera
  • Passive illumination mode
03Sampling arms+

Twin seven-axis manipulators fold into the vessel silhouette during descent, then extend for precise contact at the seabed.

  • Force-feedback control
  • Interchangeable tools
  • Sterile sample chamber
04Surface signal+

A redundant acoustic link carries low-bandwidth telemetry through kilometres of water. Critical data remains stored aboard until recovery.

  • Dual acoustic modems
  • Emergency ascent beacon
  • Local encrypted record

Interactive case file

Designed for
the scroll.

The purpose of this concept is simple: make scrolling feel like physically advancing and reversing a film—not moving between a stack of webpages.

01

Deterministic motion

Every visual state is calculated directly from document position. Scrolling upward reconstructs the same expedition in reverse.

02

Art-directed devices

Landscape and portrait screens receive separately composed artwork rather than relying on one aggressively cropped image.

03

Progressive delivery

The opening sequence loads first. Remaining scenes are prepared in the background, with a readable static experience when scripts are unavailable.

04

Inclusive by default

Reduced-motion visitors receive complete static chapter frames, while semantic headings, native controls and keyboard navigation remain available.
